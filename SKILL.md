## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small intellectual emblem, not a full manuscript page and not a modern logo. Reduce the subject to one contour, one structural relation and one memory point, then support it with a little halo, axis or engraved tick. The field is warm ivory or parchment. Ink is antique, with a scarce cobalt, cinnabar or teal accent taken from the photograph. Type is a tiny museum label. Reject a crowded codex page, a commercial icon, a tech UI, cartoon and a complete realistic object.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-288/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
